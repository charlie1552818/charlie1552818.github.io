Safety constraints
that survive reality.

A research thread on nonsmooth safe sets, bounded disturbances, learned uncertainty and optimization-based safety filters for multi-agent and nonlinear systems.

01 / PROBLEM

Safe sets are rarely smooth or isolated from uncertainty.

Practical safety filters face actuator saturation, model error, nonsmooth constraints and interaction between several simultaneously active barriers. The research target is a filter whose mathematical certificate and numerical behavior agree.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Certificate, monitor, optimization.

A

Barrier construction

Encode collision, connectivity or task safety as explicit barrier inequalities, including nonsmooth composite constraints where required.

B

Uncertainty margin

Separate learned disturbance estimates from certified safety margins instead of treating a predictor as a guarantee.

C

QP safety filter

Minimally modify nominal commands while enforcing active safety constraints and actuator limits.

D

Stress monitoring

Track active-set transitions, constraint margins and solver failure modes under deliberately difficult scenarios.

03 / EVIDENCE

Numerical stress tests.

04 / PRINCIPLE

The design rule.

Learning may improve nominal performance, but the safety claim must remain tied to an explicit certificate, a bounded uncertainty model and observable constraint margins.
